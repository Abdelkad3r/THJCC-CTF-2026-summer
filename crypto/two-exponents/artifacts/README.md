# Two Exponents (medium)

Marketing and Legal both encrypted the same internal memo. Marketing used the
public exponent they found on a sticky note, Legal used the one from the wiki.
Nobody thought to give them different moduli.

Files: `output.txt`

> Hint: the classic attack wants `gcd(e1, e2) == 1`. Check that assumption
> before you trust it.

Flag format: `THJCC{...}`
